No. 01 — The Craft

From a bare spool to a boxed keepsake.

Scroll, and watch a commission come together — the same route every piece travels through our workshop.

01The Thread/ 04
01.

Choose the thread

Two hundred and twenty shades of colourfast cotton and silk, wound in-house. Bring us a photo of the nursery — or last season's sell-out border — and we'll match the palette by eye, the way we have since 2012.

02.

Hoop the cloth

Organic muslin, bamboo terry, washed linen and panjabi cotton, hooped at hand-checked tension so nothing puckers — even after the hundredth wash.

03.

Stitch the design

Your font. Your motif. Your border code. Every commission is digitised from scratch, run at 750 stitches a minute, then inspected loop by loop before it leaves the frame.

04.

Press & parcel

Steamed on the reverse, wrapped in acid-free tissue, tied with ribbon. Gift notes are hand-lettered — never printed.
